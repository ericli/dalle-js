DALL·E Mini

A text-to-image model from 2021, running entirely on your GPU. Type a prompt and it paints a 256×256 image, one 16×16 patch at a time. Nothing you type leaves your device.

BART + VQGAN · 485M parameters · WebGPU

    How it works

    Each stage runs on your GPU. Scrub through the 256 steps, or replay them.

    —

    1. [1] Tokenizer

      The prompt is lowercased and split into subwords, between start and end markers.

    2. [2] Text encoder 12 layers

      Twelve transformer layers turn each token into 1,024 numbers, which the decoder reads at every step.

      −+first 128 of 1,024 features

    3. [3] Image decoder 12 layers · 256 steps

      Picks one patch at a time, left to right, top to bottom, from a codebook of 16,384. Each step runs twice, with and without the prompt, then exaggerates the difference.

      0 / 256
       
      16 × with prompt − 15 × without

      1. [4] VQGAN 16×16 tokens → 256×256 pixels

        Looks up each token's code and upsamples sixteen-fold into pixels. The colors are a fixed projection of its internal channels.
