    How it works

    Every stage runs on your GPU. Scrub through the 256 decoding steps, or replay them.

    —

    1. Tokenizer

      The prompt is lowercased and split into subwords from a 50,264-entry vocabulary, between start and end markers.

    2. Text encoderBART · 12 layers

      Twelve transformer layers turn the tokens into 1,024 features each, which the image decoder reads at every step.

      −+· first 128 of 1,024 features

    3. Image decoderBART · 12 layers · 256 steps

      Picks the image one 16×16-pixel patch at a time, left to right, top to bottom: 256 tokens from a 16,384-entry codebook. Each step runs twice, with and without the prompt, and pushes the result away from the unprompted guess.
